Support

If you need help using iSub Assistant Manager or have found a technical problem, please get in touch.

Email support

Support is provided by David Pritchard.

depritchard.apps@gmail.com

When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • Your app version
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The sport and screen involved
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • A screenshot, where appropriate
Please do not send passwords, player medical information, private contact details or other sensitive information.

Account and data controls

Signed-in users can change their password, export their app data or permanently delete their account from Account & privacy inside the app.

Password recovery

From the sign-in screen, select Forgot password? and follow the link sent to your registered email address. Open the recovery email on the iPhone where iSub Assistant Manager is installed.

Response times

This is an independently operated app. We aim to acknowledge support enquiries within five business days.
