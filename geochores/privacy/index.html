Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

GeoChores is developed and operated by David Pritchard. It helps a parent or guardian create family routines, pair a child’s device, review routine progress and manage selected app restrictions.

Information GeoChores stores

GeoChores stores the parent’s email address for authentication. It also stores information entered or created by the family, including parent, child and household names; household membership; routine and chore names; schedules, timers and completion status; lock commands and reasons; pairing records; and recent device-contact times.

The service also stores account and household identifiers and a notification token for the child device. These are used to keep the correct family accounts connected and deliver parent-requested notifications.

Information that remains on the child device

The precise home coordinate used for arrival monitoring is handled on the child device and is not uploaded to the GeoChores cloud database. The app may synchronise the resulting routine state or device-contact time, but not a location history.

Apple’s Family Controls framework represents selected apps, categories and websites with privacy-preserving tokens. GeoChores keeps the selection on the child device so it can apply Apple’s restrictions; the parent device does not receive a readable list of the child’s installed or selected apps.

Optional chore-evidence photos are kept on the device. GeoChores may synchronise the related chore status, but it does not upload the photo itself to its cloud database.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide GeoChores’ account, pairing, routine, progress, notification, location-trigger and app-restriction functions; maintain security; troubleshoot problems; and respond to support requests.

GeoChores does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising or use family information for cross-app tracking.

Service providers and sharing

Authentication, cloud database storage, server functions and push-notification delivery are provided using Google Firebase. Apple provides the operating-system services used for location, notifications and Family Controls. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and apply their own security and privacy obligations.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer where appropriate protections continue to apply.

Children’s information and parental control

GeoChores is designed for setup and management by a parent or guardian. A child profile is paired to a parent-managed household and does not require the child to provide an email address. Parents and guardians should enter only the information needed to use the service and are responsible for supervising its use by children.

Do not enter precise addresses into chore names, lock reasons or other free-text fields. Use the child’s first name or a nickname where practical.

Retention, deletion and choices

Cloud information is retained while it is needed to operate the account and household. A parent can delete a paired child from GeoChores settings, which removes that child’s pairing, cloud routine state, lock state, progress history, device status and child authentication account. Some short-lived technical records may remain temporarily in backups or service logs.

To request deletion of a parent account or household, or to ask for access or correction, email depritchard.apps@gmail.com from the parent account’s registered email address.

Location, notification and Family Controls permission can be changed in iOS Settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the related GeoChores feature from working. Locally stored photos and app-selection data are removed when the app’s local data is removed, subject to iOS behaviour.

Security

GeoChores uses authenticated accounts, household access rules and platform security controls intended to restrict information to the correct household. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when GeoChores or its data practices change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
